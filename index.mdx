## What makes CoreBase different

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Zero Raw Data Egress" icon="shield-check" href="/concepts/zero-raw-data-egress">

## How it works

1. **Install CoreMCP** on your own server — a small Go binary that connects to your databases.
2. **Connect to CoreBase** over an outbound-only WebSocket. We only see schema metadata.
3. **Query in natural language** from the panel, embedded widget, or the Developer API.
