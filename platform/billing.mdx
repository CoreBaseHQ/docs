CoreBase uses a **prepaid credit model** — your balance is in real US dollars, and each query deducts based on tokens consumed. Subscriptions grant a monthly included-usage allowance; top-ups add to a balance that never expires.

## How charges work

| Resource | Cost |
|---|---|
| Tokens (input + output) | $3 per million tokens |
| Subscription seat | Flat monthly fee (Pro / Team) |
| Top-up purchase | Custom amount, $5–$200, 1:1 USD → credit |

A typical "small" question (~1,000 tokens roundtrip) costs about **$0.003**. A complex analytical query with retrieval and synthesis can be **$0.02–$0.05**.

## Deduction order

When a query consumes credit:

1. The cost comes out of your **monthly grant** first (the allowance from your active subscription).
2. If that runs out, it spills into your **top-up balance** (never expires).
3. If both are empty, the API returns `402 Payment Required` and the panel surfaces an upgrade prompt.

<Tip>
  Top-up credits **never expire** and survive subscription cancellation. Monthly grants reset every billing cycle.
</Tip>

## Adding balance

In **Billing → Add Credits**, enter any amount between **$5 and $200**. You're redirected to a Stripe-hosted checkout. Once paid, your balance updates within a few seconds via webhook. The exact amount you paid (minus Stripe fees, on our side) is credited 1:1 to your top-up balance.

## Managing your subscription

Click **Manage subscription** in the billing page to open the Stripe Customer Portal. From there:

- Switch between Pro and Team
- Update payment method
- Cancel (you keep access until the period end)
- Download invoices

## Usage analytics

Per-query usage is also forwarded to Stripe's metered usage system as a `corebase.tokens.consumed` event tied to your customer ID. This means token usage shows up on your Stripe dashboard alongside the prepaid charges — no surprises on the invoice.

## Refunds

Top-up credits are non-refundable once the Stripe charge clears, but unused balance never expires. Subscription cancellations take effect at the end of the current period — no proration.
