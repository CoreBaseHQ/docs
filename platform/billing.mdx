CoreBase pricing has two parts: a **flat monthly plan** (features and limits) and a **prepaid usage balance** for AI calls. You can skip usage charges entirely by bringing your own LLM keys.

## How AI usage is billed

Every chat turn runs in one of two modes, chosen automatically:

- **Your own keys ([Bring Your Own Key](/platform/llm-keys))** — inference runs on your provider account. CoreBase adds **no per-token charges**; your balance is untouched.
- **Platform balance** — no key configured? Requests run on CoreBase's managed models and are billed from your prepaid balance at the published per-model rates.

<Note>
  **No surprise bills — ever.** The balance is prepaid: when it runs out, AI responses pause until you top up (or add your own key). There is no metered invoice at the end of the month.
</Note>

### Published rates

Requests are routed quality-first across frontier Claude models. Each turn is billed at the **published rate of the model that actually served it** — the full rate table lives in **Billing → Model pricing** in your panel. Rates include our margin; what you see is what you pay.

### What a query typically costs

Costs scale with how much work the agent does — a simple question is one model call, a deep data question can be many steps (schema lookups, queries, follow-ups). At current published rates:

| Query profile | Typical cost |
|---|---|
| Simple question, no data access | ~$0.01–0.03 |
| Typical data query (a few agent steps) | ~$0.05–0.15 |
| Deep multi-step investigation | ~$0.30–0.50+ |

Your own real numbers — average and 90th-percentile cost per query, measured from your actual usage — are shown in **Analytics → Cost**.

## Tracking spend

**Analytics → Cost** answers "what is this burning?" from real usage data:

- **This billing period** — spend aligned to your renewal date, plus a projected period total at your current pace.
- **Per team member and per widget visitor** — who is using AI, how many turns, at what cost.
- **Per model** — which models served your traffic and what each cost.
- **BYOK estimate** — using your own keys, CoreBase bills nothing; an optional toggle shows an *estimated* cost at provider list prices so you can still attribute spend internally.

When your balance runs low, the dashboard warns you before responses pause.

## Balance and top-ups

- Paid plans include monthly usage credit: **$5/month** on Growth, **$15/month** on Team. Free includes a **one-time $1 trial credit**.
- Top up any amount from **$5 to $500** in the billing page. Purchased credit **never expires**.
- Included credit is spent first, then purchased credit.

## Starting a subscription

In **Billing → Plans**, click **Switch to Growth** or **Switch to Team**. You're redirected to a secure hosted checkout. Once paid, your plan flips within a few seconds.

<Note>
  You can use the Free plan indefinitely, as long as you stay within its limits. Upgrade when you need more sources, audit logging, RBAC, or monthly usage credit.
</Note>

## Managing your subscription

Click **Manage subscription** in the billing page to open the customer portal. From there:

- Switch between Growth and Team
- Update payment method
- Cancel (you keep access until the period end)
- Download invoices

## Refunds

Subscription cancellations take effect at the end of the current period — no proration. Purchased top-up credit is non-refundable but never expires.
