CoreBase charges a flat monthly subscription. LLM calls run against your own provider API keys ([Bring Your Own Key](/platform/llm-keys)) — no per-token markup from us.

Corporate Memory — the schema annotations and business glossary that ground the agent in your data — is included in every plan. Tier differences are volume (data sources) and enterprise controls (RBAC, audit log, air-gap).

## What you pay for

| Resource | Cost |
|---|---|
| Platform subscription | Flat monthly fee (Pro / Team) |
| LLM tokens | Billed by your provider, not by CoreBase |
| Data sources | Included in plan |

## Starting a subscription

In **Billing → Plans**, click **Switch to Pro** or **Switch to Team**. You're redirected to Stripe-hosted checkout. Once paid, your plan flips within a few seconds.

<Note>
  You can use the Free plan indefinitely, as long as you stay within its limits. Upgrade when you need more sources, audit logging, or RBAC.
</Note>

## Managing your subscription

Click **Manage subscription** in the billing page to open the Stripe Customer Portal. From there:

- Switch between Pro and Team
- Update payment method
- Cancel (you keep access until the period end)
- Download invoices

## Refunds

Subscription cancellations take effect at the end of the current period — no proration.
