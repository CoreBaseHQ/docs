## Onboarding

Brand-new organizations are walked through a short setup the first time they sign in:

1. **Add an LLM key** — paste your Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, or other provider API key. Chat won't work without one.
2. **Connect a source** — paste a Postgres DSN, register a CoreMCP agent (MSSQL / Firebird), wire a REST or GraphQL API, or hook up Slack / Microsoft 365 ingestion.
3. **Set a starter policy** — pick a recipe (e.g. *lock #finance to admins*).
4. **Done** — land on the dashboard.

You can re-run any step from the corresponding page in the sidebar.

## Roles

CoreBase respects Clerk's organization roles:

| Role | Capabilities |
|---|---|
| `org:admin` | Everything — billing, policies, agents, members |
| `org:member` | Chat + view sources, scoped by access policies |

Per-feature gating beyond the two roles is done with user attributes (see [Access policies](/concepts/rbac)).
